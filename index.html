What fits an RTX 3060 12 GB for coding?

Using an RTX 3060 12 GB, 32 GB system RAM, 8K context, Balanced priority, automatic quantization and no speed minimum, the current September 2026 catalogue ranks these three highest. Memory and speed are estimates, not benchmarks.
